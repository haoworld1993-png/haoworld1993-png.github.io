執棋問鼎 隱私權政策 / ZhiQi WenDing Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 15 日 / Last updated: September 15, 2026

中文

「執棋問鼎」（以下稱本遊戲）由獨立開發者徐偉鈞（皞界 Haoworld）開發與發行。本政策說明本遊戲會使用哪些資料、為什麼使用、存放在哪裡，以及您可以怎麼選擇。

一、我們自己收集的資料（線上功能）

本遊戲提供排行榜、雲端存檔、與其他玩家陣容對戰等線上功能。為了讓這些功能運作，我們會把以下資料上傳到我們自己的伺服器：

資料用途
匿名帳號識別碼（首次啟動自動產生的一串亂數）辨識「這是同一位玩家」。不含姓名、電子郵件、電話或裝置識別碼，也無法反推出您是誰
暱稱（您自己在排行榜取的名字）顯示在排行榜與其他玩家的對戰畫面上
遊戲進度（最佳連勝、銅錢、名將收藏、戰功、教學與劇情進度、轉職設定）雲端存檔，讓您換裝置後能還原進度
出戰陣容（主帥與三位名將的名稱）提供給其他玩家在「無盡征途」中遭遇
回報內容（您在「回報問題」輸入的文字，連同遊戲版本與作業系統名稱）修正錯誤、改善遊戲
檢舉紀錄（您檢舉了哪個暱稱、原因）審核不當暱稱

我們不收集：姓名、電子郵件、電話、地址、精確位置、通訊錄、照片、裝置序號或廣告識別碼（廣告識別碼由 AdMob 處理，見第二節）。

不需要註冊。匿名帳號在您第一次開啟遊戲時自動建立，不需要提供任何資料。

帳號綁定（自願，v0.7.0 起）：您可以在「玩家資訊」把匿名帳號綁定到 Google 或 Apple 帳號，用途只有一個——換裝置時把雲端進度接回來。綁定時我們會從 Google／Apple 收到您的電子郵件地址（Apple 使用者可選擇隱藏真實信箱），它只儲存在我們的帳號系統中用來辨識同一位玩家，不會顯示給其他玩家、不寄送任何信件、不用於行銷。不綁定完全不影響遊戲；綁定後隨時可在同一畫面「登出，回到匿名」，或用「刪除帳號」把資料連同信箱一併刪除。

內購（v0.7.0 起）：遊戲提供一項買斷型商品「軍師令」。付款由 Google Play／App Store 處理，我們不會收到您的信用卡或付款資料，只會從商店得知「這個帳號是否已購買」以便解鎖功能。

資料存放：由 Supabase Inc. 提供的資料庫服務代管，伺服器位於日本東京。Supabase 是我們的資料處理者，僅依我們的指示儲存資料，不會將資料用於其他目的。Supabase 隱私權政策：https://supabase.com/privacy

誰看得到：

  • 排行榜上的暱稱與最佳連勝：所有玩家都看得到
  • 出戰陣容（主帥與名將名稱）與暱稱：會出現在其他玩家的遊戲中作為對手
  • 雲端存檔、回報內容、檢舉紀錄：只有開發者本人看得到，其他玩家無法讀取

離線也能玩：沒有網路時遊戲照常運作，只是排行榜與雲端存檔暫時不可用。

二、廣告（由第三方提供）

本遊戲使用 Google AdMob 顯示獎勵型廣告。廣告只在您主動點選「看廣告」時播放（補體力、還魂、賞金翻倍、更換戰利品），不會在遊戲過程中強制插入。

為了投放與計算廣告，AdMob 可能會收集：

  • 廣告識別碼（Android 的 Advertising ID／iOS 的 IDFA，需經您同意）
  • 裝置型號、作業系統版本、語言、時區
  • IP 位址（用於大致地區判斷）
  • 廣告互動紀錄（是否看完、是否點擊）

這些資料由 Google 依其隱私權政策處理，我們無法取得可識別您個人的資料。

  • Google 隱私權政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google 廣告技術合作夥伴：https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

您的選擇

  • iOS：系統會詢問是否允許追蹤（App Tracking Transparency），拒絕後只會看到非個人化廣告。
  • Android：可在「設定 → Google → 廣告」重設或刪除廣告 ID。
  • 歐盟／英國／瑞士：首次啟動會出現同意視窗，可拒絕個人化廣告。

三、您的權利

改名：隨時可在排行榜畫面更改暱稱。

刪除帳號：遊戲內「中軍帳 → 刪除帳號」會立即刪除伺服器上屬於您的全部資料（匿名帳號、雲端存檔、排行榜紀錄、陣容、暱稱）。回報內容會保留文字但去除與您帳號的關聯。這台裝置上的本機進度不受影響。刪除後無法復原。

檢舉：排行榜上每個暱稱旁都有「檢舉」按鈕。不當暱稱經審核後會被移除或更改。

查詢或其他請求：來信 haoworld1993@gmail.com，我們會在 14 天內回覆。

四、資料保留

  • 帳號、存檔、排行榜、陣容：保留至您刪除帳號為止
  • 回報內容：處理完畢後保留最多 1 年，供追蹤同類問題
  • 檢舉紀錄：保留最多 1 年

五、網路連線用途

本遊戲連線只用於：線上功能（第一節）、廣告（第二節）、校正時間（體力回復計時，只讀取伺服器時間）。

六、兒童

本遊戲不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會刻意向兒童收集資料。若您是家長且認為孩子提供了資料，請來信，我們會協助刪除。

七、政策更新

本政策若有變更，會在此頁面與應用程式商店頁面公告，重大變更會在遊戲內提示。

English

ZhiQi WenDing ("the Game") is developed and published by independent developer Wei-Chun Hsu (Haoworld). This policy explains what data the Game uses, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect ourselves (online features)

The Game offers leaderboards, cloud save, and battles against other players' line-ups. To make these work, we upload the following to our own server:

DataPurpose
Anonymous account ID (a random string generated on first launch)Recognize the same player. Contains no name, email, phone, or device identifier, and cannot be traced back to you
Nickname (the name you choose for the leaderboard)Shown on the leaderboard and in other players' battles
Game progress (best streak, coins, general collection, feats, tutorial/story progress, class changes)Cloud save, so you can restore progress on a new device
Line-up (names of your lord and three generals)Served to other players as an opponent in Endless mode
Feedback (text you type in "Report a problem", plus game version and OS name)Fixing bugs, improving the Game
Reports (which nickname you reported and why)Moderating inappropriate nicknames

We do not collect: name, email, phone, address, precise location, contacts, photos, device serial numbers, or advertising IDs (advertising IDs are handled by AdMob, see section 2).

No registration. The anonymous account is created automatically on first launch; you never enter any information.

Account linking (optional, from v0.7.0): In "Player Info" you may link your anonymous account to a Google or Apple account, solely so that your cloud progress can be restored on another device. When you link, we receive your email address from Google/Apple (Apple users may choose to hide their real address). It is stored only in our account system to recognize the same player; it is never shown to other players, never used to send mail, and never used for marketing. Linking is entirely optional; you can "Sign out, back to anonymous" on the same screen at any time, or use "Delete Account" to erase all data including the email.

In-app purchase (from v0.7.0): The Game offers one one-time product, "Advisor Pass". Payment is handled by Google Play / the App Store; we never receive your card or payment details, only whether this store account owns the item so we can unlock it.

Storage: hosted by Supabase Inc. on servers in Tokyo, Japan. Supabase is our data processor and stores data only on our instructions. Supabase privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

Who can see it:

  • Nickname and best streak on the leaderboard: all players
  • Line-up (lord and general names) with nickname: appears in other players' games as an opponent
  • Cloud save, feedback, reports: only the developer; no other player can read them

Offline play: the Game works without a connection; only leaderboard and cloud save are temporarily unavailable.

2. Advertising (provided by third parties)

The Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads. Ads play only when you tap a "Watch ad" button (refill stamina, revive, double a reward, reroll loot). No ads are forced during gameplay.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect: advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, subject to your consent), device model, OS version, language, time zone, IP address (approximate region), and ad interaction data. This is processed by Google under its own policy; we never receive data that identifies you.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google ad technology partners: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

Your choices: iOS asks for tracking permission (ATT); Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Google → Ads; EU/UK/Switzerland users see a consent dialog on first launch.

3. Your rights

Rename: change your nickname any time from the leaderboard screen.

Delete account: in-game "Camp → Delete Account" immediately deletes all your server-side data (anonymous account, cloud save, leaderboard entry, line-up, nickname). Feedback text is kept but unlinked from your account. Local progress on this device is unaffected. Deletion cannot be undone.

Report: every nickname on the leaderboard has a "Report" button. Inappropriate nicknames are removed or changed after review.

Access or other requests: email haoworld1993@gmail.com; we reply within 14 days.

4. Retention

  • Account, save, leaderboard, line-up: until you delete your account
  • Feedback: up to 1 year after it is handled
  • Reports: up to 1 year

5. Network use

The Game connects only for: online features (section 1), ads (section 2), and a time check for stamina regeneration (reads server time only).

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Parents who believe a child has provided data may contact us for deletion.

7. Changes

Changes are posted on this page and the store listing; material changes are also noted in the Game.
